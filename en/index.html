Positioning and personality

Photography10

Photography direction

Soft, diffused light, clean backgrounds from the palette and a warm grade with identity accents: approachable, polished beauty with doodle winks.

Beauty / skincare

Close-ups of real skin, textures (masks, serum), window light, pink or ivory backgrounds. Warm pink grade.

Self-care

Slow rituals at home: towels, steam, matcha, hands and gestures. Calm composition with a single colour accent.

Product and flat-lay

Plain palette background, integrated doodle overlays and a single light source. The product always readable.

Layout11

Composition system

Rule 01
Margins

At least 8% on the sides; critical content within the central 80%.

Rule 02
Grid

Alternate A blocks (photo-led) and B blocks (type-led).

Rule 03
Hierarchy

Hook of six words maximum → fact → CTA. One script per design.

Rule 04
Air

At least 20% white space; no more than two accents at once.

Rule 05
Ratios

Feed 4:5 · reels and stories 9:16 · flat-lays 1:1.

Rule 06
Repeated stamp

Wavy line or stamp on at least 6 of every 9 posts: identity without a logo.

Brand guidelines22

Correct and incorrect use

Do

✓ One handwritten accent per design — max rotation −3°

✓ Dark headlines on pastel backgrounds — readable on mobile

✓ Alternate photo and type blocks in the feed — editorial rhythm

✓ Hopebeauty watermark on product photos — subtle protection

Don’t

✕ Electric blue and lilac together in the foreground — clashing accents

✕ More than two accents per slide — visual noise

✕ Script in long texts — it is an accent, not body copy

✕ Small white text on bubblegum pink — use ink or the deep variant
